# XXD Shop 008 · Original brief (English reading translation)

> Reading translation only. The canonical runtime prompt is [zh-CN.md](zh-CN.md).

From the product image supplied by the user, generate a commercial product poster in a miniature brick-world style.

First accurately identify the product's category, outline, packaging structure, main colours, brand information, text layout and core visual features. Strictly keep the product's own recognisability so it is known at a glance as the original product, but turn the whole product into "a giant product body assembled from standard plastic brick pieces".

The image uses a vertical commercial-advertising composition. The product is the absolute centre and largest subject, like a brick sculpture or landmark building enlarged to city scale, standing firmly in the middle of the miniature scene. The product must be complete, clear and naturally proportioned, keeping the original packaging information while clearly having a brick-built texture.

Build a complete miniature construction-and-display world around the product: brick scaffolding, maintenance platforms, lifting structures, hoisting devices, engineering supports or other reasonable construction systems around it; several miniature brick workers busy carrying, assembling, polishing, inspecting, hoisting, wiping, maintaining, posting and patrolling, giving the image narrative and energy.

From the product's character and category, naturally extend a suitable miniature scene base without mechanically applying templates: roads, platforms, display bases, city facilities, landscape greenery, auxiliary buildings, machinery, storage units, vehicles or theme environments related to the product, so the whole world feels like a dedicated miniature space built around this giant product. All scene elements serve the product and never steal the show.

Apart from the sky, air or a very small amount of background atmosphere, the subject, workers, scaffolding, machinery, roads, buildings, trees, devices and environmental details should all appear as standard plastic bricks assembled together. Emphasise modular studs, assembled structure, clutch logic, brick corners, faceted divisions and the orderly beauty of a toy model, while keeping the whole refined, complete and richly detailed — not rough, not messy.

Material is high-quality ABS plastic, mostly matte, with clean surfaces, crisp brick edges and natural, delicate local reflections. Light is bright and natural with soft dimensional shading, giving the giant product, workers and miniature scene clear volume and commercial-advertising quality.

Colour unfolds from the product's original palette, so the whole miniature world builds a unified order around the product's colours. Background and supporting elements may be rich but must be controlled in hierarchy, ensuring the first glance sees the giant product and the second glance the construction narrative and miniature details.

The overall style combines the fun of a miniature world, the refinement of brick building, the clear recognisability of commercial product advertising, and high-completion 3D render quality. The image is clean, complete and rich but not cluttered, suitable for e-commerce main images, brand creative key visuals and product promotion posters.

Avoid: reduced product recognisability, blurry product text, a subject drowned by the scene, chaotic miniature elements, piles of irrelevant buildings, distorted proportions, a cheap toy feel, a childish cartoon feel, a background that steals the show, and construction elements blocking the product's key information.
